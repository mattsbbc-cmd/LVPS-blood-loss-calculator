Calibrate swabs

Do this before first use, and every time a new box or brand of swab arrives. It takes about two minutes.

    Start calibration

    1. Choose the swab type

    2. Zero the scale

      Switch the scale on with nothing on it. Make sure it reads 0.0 g. If it doesn't, press tare or zero.

    3. Count out 10 clean swabs

      Take them from an unopened or freshly opened pack. They must be completely dry and unused. Don't include the packaging or any X-ray strip that isn't part of the swab.

    4. Weigh them together

      Put all the swabs on the scale at once. Wait for the reading to settle, then type it in.

    5. Check and save

      Method and references

      ABL = EBV × (PCVstart − PCVmin) ÷ ((PCVstart + PCVmin) ÷ 2)

      The allowable blood loss formula was derived in human patients. Gravimetric measurement has been validated in dogs. This tool supports clinical judgement and does not replace it. Version 1.5, October 2026.
